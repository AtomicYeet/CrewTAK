How Crew Users Work

1

Crew admins add users here and can reset their passwords or remove them.

2

Users sign in with just their username and password.

3

Pick a shift and a role (Crew, Manager or Admin) when you add someone. Change either later from their row.

4

Admins can manage Crew Users. Managers can approve, edit and delete anyone's tasks.
